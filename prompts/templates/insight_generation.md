## Instructions
Generate a structured supply chain insight following this format:
- **Finding**: What the data shows
- **Impact**: Business impact assessment (High/Medium/Low)
- **Root Cause Hypothesis**: Likely contributing factors
- **Recommended Action**: Specific, actionable recommendation for human review
- **Confidence Score**: Your confidence level (0-100%) with reasoning
- **Data Sources**: Which data was used
